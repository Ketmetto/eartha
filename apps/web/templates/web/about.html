WHY EARTHA EXISTS

The future of fashion isn't about making more — it's about making what already exists last longer. Eartha brings together people, brands, and circular solutions to extend the life of clothing, reduce waste, and reward the choices that create lasting impact.

HOW IT WORKS

FIVE STEPS TO A SECOND LIFE.

01

UPLOAD

Upload an unused item in good condition — add photos, select its category and condition, then submit it for review.

02

EARN CREDITS

EARTHA verifies the item and assigns credits based on category and condition. Approved credits land in your EARTHA Wallet.

03

REDEEM REWARDS

Browse the EARTHA Marketplace and redeem credits with partner brands, cafés, wellness studios, and fashion stores.

04

DROP OFF ITEM

Drop off the approved item at an EARTHA collection point, or hand it to a nearby partner location for easy pickup.

05

CREATE IMPACT

Your item then re-enters EARTHA's circular economy — redirected for resale, donation, upcycling, or recycling.
